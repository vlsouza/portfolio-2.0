## The Project

### Gameplay

Explore a whimsical world, dodge enemy frogs and collect gems through levels I designed.

### Mechanics

- **Movement:** fluid controls, jump and double jump.
- **Collectibles:** gems and extra health items that reward exploration.

### Key features

- Parallax backgrounds for a more immersive world.
- Built in Unity with C#, using the "Fox's Tale" asset pack.

### What I learned

- **Pacing:** balancing intensity and player engagement.
- **Signposting and landmarks:** guiding players and clarifying objectives.
- **Breadcrumbing:** encouraging exploration.
- **One-way gates:** a structured flow and a sense of progression.
