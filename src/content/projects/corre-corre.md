## The Project

### Gameplay

Run as far as you can, jumping and sliding past obstacles, while the score goes up.

### Mechanics

- Jump and slide
- Scoring system
- Title and ending screens

### What I learned

It demystified game development for me: the basic building blocks of every game loop, applied end to end.
